02 / PHENOMENON

Not one light.
Not one description.

The testimonies describe different forms and behaviours of the lights. The descriptions below are categories from the Project Hessdalen materials, not proof of a single nature of the phenomenon.

OBSERVATION CATALOGUE01 — 03

HOW IT APPEARS IN THE DESCRIPTIONS

Testimonies
of different kinds.

Some reports speak of a short flash. Others — of a luminous form that moved slowly or stayed almost motionless. Sometimes several lights were described together.

In the project’s older materials these accounts are grouped into several recurring types. Such a classification helps compare observations — but by itself it does not explain what caused them.

!Not every report is automatically classified as an unexplained phenomenon: details, context and the quality of the record matter.

TYPES OF DESCRIPTIONS

Three forms of testimony

The visual marks are a diagram, not a reconstruction.
TYPE 01

A short flash

A small bright white or bluish light that quickly appears in the sky and disappears.

instantaneouspoint source
TYPE 02

A single light

A white or yellow light of varying shape — a sphere, an oval or an elongated figure. In some accounts it could stay in place for a long time or move slowly.

singlevariable duration
TYPE 03

A group of lights

Several sources positioned close together or moving in a coordinated way. Sometimes a red light was mentioned among white or yellow ones.

several sourcespattern of movement

AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

What was seen —
and what it means.

The phrase “a light was observed” conveys the impression of an eyewitness. To move to an explanation, measurements and testing of alternatives are needed.
