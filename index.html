01 / CONTEXT

A mystery
they chose to measure.

Hessdalen is a small valley in central Norway. Since late 1981 residents have reported unusual lights in the sky and above the slopes. In the early 1980s such reports became especially frequent.

This dossier separates recorded facts from assumptions: what exactly was seen, how it was measured, and why the phenomenon still has no generally accepted explanation.
