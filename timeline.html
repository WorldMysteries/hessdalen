The story of Hessdalen spans several decades of reports, fieldwork and attempts to observe the phenomenon systematically.

The chronology is based on the historical summary of Project Hessdalen. The periods are not a complete record of all observations.

SHOW
DECEMBER 1981
OBSERVATIONS

The lights become a notable topic in the valley

Residents begin reporting unknown lights. In the following years the frequency of reports rises sharply.

01
3 JUNE 1983
PROJECT

Project Hessdalen is founded

Researchers join forces to systematically collect reports and study the phenomenon on site.

02
21 JANUARY — 26 FEBRUARY 1984
FIELD CAMPAIGN

Five weeks of observations and measurements

During the expedition 53 light observations were registered. Several kinds of instruments were used; the results went into a technical report.

WINTER 1985
FIELD CAMPAIGN

The second expedition

The second fieldwork took place in winter. According to the project summary, no phenomena were observed while the instruments were in place.

04
AUGUST 1998
AUTOMATION

The measuring station is installed

The Hessdalen Automatic Measurement Station (AMS) was placed in the valley to record events and collect data automatically.
